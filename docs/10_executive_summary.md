# Executive Summary

The claims operations assistant is a decision-support system designed to improve claims intake, review, and approval throughput in a healthcare context. It helps analysts triage claims more efficiently, keeps human review in the loop, and provides operational visibility through dashboards and audit logs.

## Value Delivered
- faster case intake
- clearer visibility into open workload
- safer handling of sensitive information
- consistent status tracking and audit quality
- decision support with measurable release readiness checks

## Why It Matters
Healthcare claims operations are high-volume and sensitive. The system addresses operational friction without sacrificing accountability or governance. It demonstrates how AI can augment a human workflow in a realistic, trustworthy, and measurable way.

## Final Positioning
This project is a strong candidate for an applied engineering or forward-deployed engineering portfolio because it demonstrates product thinking, workflow design, safety discipline, and measurable outcomes.
